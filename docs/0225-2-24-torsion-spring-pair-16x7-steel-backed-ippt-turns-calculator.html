Frequently Asked Questions (0.225 × 2" × 24" Torsion Spring Pair (16x7 Steel-Backed))

How much garage door weight does a 0.225 × 2" × 24" torsion spring lift?

Each 0.225" × 2" × 24" spring has a torque rate of 0.09 IPPT (Inch-Pounds Per Turn). On standard 4-inch drums (2.0" radius) wound to 7.5 turns (7-ft door), a single spring balances a 0-lb door, and a matched pair balances a 1-lb door.

How many quarter-turns do I wind a torsion spring on a 7-foot garage door?

For a standard 7-foot tall door on 4-inch drums, wind each spring 7.5 full turns (30 quarter-turns). For an 8-foot tall door, wind each spring 8.5 full turns (34 quarter-turns). Paint a straight chalk line across the relaxed spring coils before winding so you can count full revolutions visually.

How do I measure my broken torsion spring to confirm it is 0.225" wire?

Do not measure a single wire strand with a tape measure! Instead, press 20 relaxed coils tightly together and measure from the 1st coil to the 20th coil. A 20-coil length of 4.5 inches (or 2.25" across 10 coils) confirms 0.225-inch wire.
